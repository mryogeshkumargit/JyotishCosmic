## 2. FUNDAMENTAL PREDICTION MODEL
A prediction is represented as:

`Prediction = Natal Promise × Strength × Activation × Transit/Timing Confirmation × Context`

Where:

- **Natal Promise** = houses, lords, occupants, yogas, conjunctions, aspects and significators.
- **Strength** = dignity, Shadbala, Bhāva Bala, Varga condition and relevant supporting factors.
- **Activation** = Mahādaśā/Antardaśā/Pratyantardaśā and other configured periods.
- **Transit Confirmation** = relevant slow/fast transits and exact contacts.
- **Context** = Lagna, Moon, relevant Bhāva, relevant Varga and current period.

A prediction should not be generated merely because one planet occupies one house.

## 5. BHĀVA EVIDENCE MODEL
For a target Bhāva H, inspect:

1. Bhāva itself
2. Bhāva lord
3. Planet(s) occupying H
4. Planet(s) aspecting H
5. Natural significator/Kāraka
6. Dispositor chain
7. Relevant conjunctions
8. Relevant Vargas
9. Bhāva Bala
10. Daśā lords connected to H
11. Transit activation of H/lord/Kāraka
12. Ashtakavarga support where configured

Phaladīpikā's Bhāva methodology explicitly examines the Bhāva lord, occupant, aspects, strength and relevant Daśā periods.

## 6. BHĀVA LORD CHAIN
For each target Bhāva:

`Bhāva → Bhāveśa → Bhāveśa's sign → Dispositor → Dispositor's house → Dispositor's strength`

Store the complete chain.

Example:

`10th Bhāva → 10th lord → 10th lord in 7th → 7th lord → 7th lord in 11th`

This creates an auditable causal chain rather than a one-line interpretation.

## 7. CONJUNCTION SYNTHESIS
For every conjunction found in the natal chart:

`Cluster → all pairwise edges → exact separations → direct multi-planet rule → Bhāva → Rāśi → dispositor → functional lordship → dignity → aspects → Vargas → Shadbala → Daśā`

A three-planet cluster creates three pairwise evidence edges.

A four-planet cluster creates six.

A five-planet cluster creates ten.

The engine preserves each edge independently before synthesizing the cluster.

## 9. PROMISE VS TIMING
This is one of the most important controls in Volume 5.

### Natal chart
Answers:

**'What is promised or structurally indicated?'**

### Daśā
Answers:

**'When is the natal configuration activated?'**

### Transit
Answers:

**'When is the activated theme externally stimulated or timed?'**

The engine must not confuse these three layers.

## 10. DAŚĀ ACTIVATION ENGINE
A Daśā period receives activation evidence when its lord:

- owns the target Bhāva,
- occupies the target Bhāva,
- aspects the target Bhāva,
- is joined with the target Bhāva lord,
- is the relevant Kāraka,
- participates in the target conjunction,
- occupies a relevant Varga position,
- is linked through dispositorship,
- or receives strong transit activation.

Phaladīpikā's Daśā chapters explicitly distinguish the effects of house lords according to their placement, dignity, strength and associations.

## 11. DAŚĀ × ANTARDĀŚĀ INTERACTION
For Mahādaśā lord M and Antardaśā lord A:

Evaluate:

1. M's natal promise
2. A's natal promise
3. M–A relationship
4. M's house ownership
5. A's house ownership
6. M/A conjunction or aspect
7. M/A dispositor relationship
8. M/A dignity
9. M/A Varga confirmation
10. M/A Shadbala
11. target Bhāva activation
12. transit confirmation

The engine therefore produces:

`MD evidence + AD evidence + relationship evidence + timing evidence`

rather than interpreting the Antardaśā planet in isolation.

## 12. TRANSIT CONFIRMATION
Transit is a **confirmation/timing layer**, not a substitute for natal promise.

For a target event domain, inspect:

- transit of relevant Daśā lord,
- transit of Bhāva lord,
- transit over natal Bhāva,
- transit over natal Bhāva lord,
- transit over conjunction,
- transit to Kāraka,
- transit to Moon/Lagna,
- slow-planet transit,
- retrograde re-contact,
- exact aspect/conjunction,
- Ashtakavarga support where available.

Phaladīpikā explicitly connects the transit of a Daśā planet through its own, exaltation or friendly sign with the strengthening of the Bhāva it represents, subject to natal strength.

## 13. ASHTAKAVARGA CONFIRMATION
Where Ashtakavarga is available, the engine stores:

- Bhāva bindu count
- relevant planet's Bhinna Ashtakavarga
- Sarvashtakavarga total
- transit sign support
- transit timing
- Shodhya Pinda where implemented

Ashtakavarga is a separate evidence stream, not an adjustment hidden inside a planetary score. BPHS has a dedicated results chapter describing benefic dots and Shodhya Pinda as determinants of favourable/unfavourable transit results.

## 16. CONFLICT RESOLUTION
When indicators disagree, the engine must expose the disagreement.

Example:

- 10th lord strong → support
- 10th Bhāva afflicted → obstruction
- D10 supportive → confirmation
- current Daśā unrelated → weak activation
- transit supportive → temporary activation

Output:

`PROMISE: supported`
`OBSTRUCTION: present`
`TIMING: not strongly activated`
`CONFIRMATION: partial`

Do **not** collapse this into an unexplained yes/no result.

## 17. EVENT CONVERGENCE RULE
A high-confidence traditional prediction should require convergence from multiple independent layers.

Suggested evidence structure:

### Level A — Natal promise
At least one major structural indication.

### Level B — Strength
Relevant planet/Bhāva is sufficiently supported or the weakness is explicitly documented.

### Level C — Daśā activation
Current period activates the same domain.

### Level D — Transit confirmation
Current transit independently activates the domain.

### Level E — Varga confirmation
Relevant divisional chart supports the same domain where applicable.

### Level F — Ashtakavarga/supporting timing
Optional additional confirmation.

The engine should report which levels are present rather than assigning an opaque confidence percentage.

|---|---|

| `NATAL_PROMISE_WEAK` | Relevant indication exists but support is limited. |
| `NATAL_CONTRADICTION` | Significant opposing natal indicators exist. |
| `TIMING_ACTIVE` | Current Daśā activates relevant factors. |
| `TRANSIT_CONFIRMED` | Transit independently activates the domain. |
| `VARGA_CONFIRMED` | Relevant Varga supports the indication. |
| `PARTIAL_CONVERGENCE` | Several layers agree but one or more are absent/conflicting. |
| `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` | Calculation cannot be completed. |

These are **technical evidence states**, not guaranteed-event labels.

## 35. PREDICTION LANGUAGE ENGINE
The output should use evidence-calibrated language.

### Strong convergence
- "The chart contains multiple converging indications..."
- "The active period directly connects..."
- "The timing is reinforced by..."

### Partial convergence
- "The chart indicates the theme, but the timing support is partial..."
- "Several factors support X, while Y modifies the result..."

### Conflicting evidence
- "The chart contains both supporting and obstructing indicators..."
- "The result is conditional on..."

### Insufficient evidence
- "The available data do not establish this reliably..."

Avoid deterministic wording such as 'this will definitely happen'.
